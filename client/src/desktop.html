Play offline

No internet or account password needed. Characters and Story saves stay on this PC.

Hosting opens the configured game port. Friends need your reachable address, then your Story join code. Saves stay on this PC.
3–24 letters, numbers or underscores.

Join a server

Use the host’s address. Accounts and characters belong to that server.

Story join codes are entered after connecting to the host’s server.

Host & create

Run a community server with its own saves, maps and accounts.

Configure the address, port and world folder in community-server.json. Publish edits in Studio, then restart your server.

Where are my saves?

Offline profiles are separate from online characters. Keep backups of this folder. Closing a host window saves and stops its server.
